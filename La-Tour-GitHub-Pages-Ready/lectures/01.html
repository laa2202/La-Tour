THE FIRST QUESTION

Natural gas inventories unexpectedly fall. What happens next?

Choose before discussing. Assume the surprise is genuinely new to the market.

RESEARCH QUESTION / DISCUSSION02 / 07
WHAT A SIGNAL IS NOT

Correlation is not a research hypothesis.

Mechanism

Why should this information change expected future prices?

Timing

Was the data observable before the trade was placed?

Implementation

Does the effect survive delays, trading costs and risk controls?

THREE TESTS OF A CREDIBLE IDEA03 / 07
THE PIPELINE

Observation → model → trade.

physical data → surprise / estimate → forecast → target exposure → realized P&L

Each arrow is a research problem. A good strategy must justify all of them.

SHARED RESEARCH ARCHITECTURE04 / 07
OUR DESK

Three quantitative mandates. One fund.

01 / Physical

Energy flows, storage, congestion and regional spreads.

02 / Forecasting

Statistical models, regimes and uncertainty estimates.

03 / Trading

Position sizing, risk overlays and consistent backtests.

Research leads own experiments. Both Co-PMs oversee all three sleeves and review the integrated result.

STRUCTURE / SHARED STANDARDS05 / 07
YOUR FIRST EXPERIMENT

Same question. Different depth.

Foundation

Inspect data, plot variables and explain the economic mechanism.

Implementation

Code a baseline signal and compute lagged strategy returns.

Advanced

Challenge timing assumptions, parameter choices and out-of-sample stability.

All analysts contribute to one reproducible experiment, regardless of experience level.

WEEK 01 / ALL LEVELS06 / 07
THE RESEARCH CONTRACT

Show the work. Challenge the result.

Hypothesis

Write it before inspecting a performance chart.

Evidence

Document data availability, methodology and failures.

Decision

Advance, revise or reject based on validation.

A failed hypothesis with good methodology is better than an unrepeatable backtest.
