# La Tour investment desk research review — 6-slide template
1. **Hypothesis / Why this should work** — precise prediction, underlying macro mechanism.
2. **Market + dataset** — instruments, fields, source, date range, adjusted vs tradeable prices, known limitations.
3. **Algorithm** — formula/pseudocode; when signals become observable; execution assumptions.
4. **Evidence** — benchmark, train/validation/test table, curves and risk metrics.
5. **Robustness / What failed** — costs, parameters, dates, implementation pitfalls, counterexamples.
6. **Investment decision** — go/no-go for continued research; unresolved questions and next experiment.

**Presentation duration:** 8 minutes + 4 minutes skeptical Q&A. No unverified returns shown as investment performance.
